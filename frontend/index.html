06 // TRANSPARENCY & DETAILS

A few things
worth knowing.

Clear, straightforward answers about our website packages, technical setup, and ongoing operations.

What is included in each package?
Every tier includes domain & hosting setup assistance, responsive mobile-first engineering, clean semantic markup, security best practices, and the exact deliverables listed in the package breakdown above.
Is domain registration included?
We provide complete setup assistance and DNS configuration at no extra cost. Domain registration fees are paid directly to your chosen registrar (e.g., Cloudflare, Namecheap, GoDaddy) so you retain 100% legal ownership of your business domain.
Are hosting renewals included?
Hosting setup assistance is provided. Ongoing server subscriptions are managed on your preferred hosting account or can be handled as part of our monthly support plans for completely hands-off maintenance.
Can I upgrade my package later?
Yes. You can ask to move to Growth or Pro as your needs change. We’ll review the additional scope and price before starting.
What are the monthly support plans?
Our Care (₹999/mo), Growth (₹2,999/mo), and Automation (₹4,999/mo) plans offer total peace of mind with continuous software updates, security monitoring, regular backups, and ongoing refinements without requiring you to hire an internal webmaster.
Are CRM and WhatsApp API subscriptions included?
Setup, architectural wiring, and integration into your website are fully included in the Pro package. Any recurring external third-party API or software subscription fees (e.g. WhatsApp Business Cloud API, CRM licenses) are billed directly by their respective providers.
How long does delivery take?
Turnaround depends on the agreed scope, content readiness and review schedule. We discuss a delivery schedule before work begins, with milestones for multi-page projects or integrations where appropriate.
Who provides website content and images?
You provide your core business details, services, contact information, and business photos. We provide professional content structure guidance, styling refinement, and high-resolution royalty-free asset curation where required.
