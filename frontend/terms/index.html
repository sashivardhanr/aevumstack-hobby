01

Services & Project Scope

AEVUM STACK provides web engineering, bespoke frontend architecture, performance tuning, and digital growth infrastructure. Services are delivered according to agreed specifications set forth in our published website packages or custom written project proposals:

  • Starter Package (₹4,999): Single-page responsive site, WhatsApp integration, contact form, Google Maps, and basic SEO setup.
  • Business Package (₹9,999): Up to 5 pages, dedicated service pages, testimonials, gallery, lead capture, and performance speed optimization.
  • Growth Package (₹14,999): Conversion-calibrated architecture, appointment/booking forms, analytics integration, and advanced local SEO.
  • Pro Package (₹29,999): Full digital infrastructure, CRM setup, lead pipeline integration, WhatsApp automation, and dashboard reporting.

Any deliverables, third-party integrations, or custom modules not explicitly listed in the agreed package brief are considered outside project scope and will require an approved written change order.

All prices published on our website are transparent, fixed investment rates quoted in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹) for standard packages.

Written proposals and custom scope estimates are governed by terms, validity periods, and scheduling provisions defined in the applicable written client agreement or quotation document.

04

Project Timelines & Schedules

Estimated turnaround times are established in our initial proposal. Timelines are targets calculated in working business days (Monday through Saturday, excluding official public holidays).

Timelines are contingent upon prompt client feedback, timely content provision, and prompt sign-offs. Delays in receiving required client assets or credentials will automatically extend project milestones accordingly.

05

Client Responsibilities

Smooth delivery requires cooperative engagement. The client agrees to:

  • Provide high-resolution branding assets (vector logos, brand colors, imagery) and accurate text content in a timely manner.
  • Provide necessary domain registrar, hosting, and API access credentials required for deployment.
  • Designate a primary decision-maker with authority to provide sprint reviews and milestone approvals.
  • Warrant that all materials, media, and text supplied to AEVUM STACK do not infringe upon any third-party copyrights or trademarks.
06

Revisions & Scope Adjustments

Each project package includes defined revision rounds to fine-tune typography, spacing, imagery, and text alignment within the agreed wireframe architecture.

Fundamental shifts in architectural direction, additional page requests, or new third-party integrations requested after scope sign-off will be quoted as separate work items.

  • Portfolio Rights: Terms governing the display of delivered work in studio case studies and professional portfolios are set forth in the applicable written client agreement.

Direct Billing Policy: Domain registration fees, DNS management costs, cloud hosting charges, third-party email services, paid API endpoints, and CRM subscription licenses are billed directly to the client by their chosen service providers (e.g. Namecheap, Cloudflare, Google Workspace).

AEVUM STACK assists with setup, DNS configuration, and performance connection, but is not a reseller, billing intermediary, or guarantor for third-party hosting uptime, API deprecations, or provider pricing changes.

Monthly Support Plans

Ongoing site health and technical care are provided via dedicated monthly support agreements:

  • Growth Plan (₹2,999/mo): Search engine visibility tune-ups, performance reports, and landing page refinement.
  • Automation Plan (₹4,999/mo): Continuous CRM maintenance, webhook checks, and automated pipeline improvements.

Support plans operate on a recurring monthly cycle, billed in advance. Minimum term commitments, renewal provisions, and cancellation notice periods will be defined in the applicable written client agreement.
