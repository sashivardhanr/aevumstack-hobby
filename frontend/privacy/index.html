LEGAL & COMPLIANCE // 01

Privacy Policy

Transparent disclosure of what client information is collected through our digital enquiry channels, how it is handled, and our operational data practices.

STATUS: OPERATIONAL DRAFT • LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 2025 • SUBJECT TO OWNER CONFIRMATION
Notice for Business Ownership & Legal Review This operational draft describes the current local V1 website and its enquiry flow. Items that require business-owner or legal review are marked with [Requires Owner Confirmation]. This draft does not establish a retention schedule or claim legal certification.
01

Scope & Overview

AEVUM STACK (aevumstack.dev) is a web engineering and digital growth studio. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent data handling practices for visitors, prospective clients, and partners who submit project briefs or initiate dialogue through our official website.

We adhere to a principle of data minimization: we only request the specific technical and commercial details necessary to evaluate your project scope, provide accurate pricing quotations, and communicate directly regarding web development engagements.

02

Information Collected Through the Contact Form

When you submit an enquiry through our direct intake form on aevumstack.dev#contact, we collect only the explicit fields provided by you:

  • Full Name: Used to address you in direct project correspondence.
  • Business Name: Used to understand your commercial archetype and evaluate industry-specific design/performance requirements.
  • Email Address or Phone Number: Used as your designated direct channel for transmitting project proposals, timelines, and discovery calls.
  • Selected Website Package or Support Plan: Used to determine your target tier (Starter, Business, Growth, Pro, or Monthly Support).
  • Project Description & Goals: Technical requirements, target audience, deliverable expectations, and milestone goals provided in your brief.
  • Submission Timestamp: Automatically generated at the moment of transmission for intake queue sequencing.
Payment & Financial Information: We do not collect credit card numbers, bank account credentials, or direct financial payment data through this website. Third-party infrastructure costs (such as domain registration or cloud hosting) are paid directly by you to their respective vendors.
03

Why We Collect This Information

Your inquiry information is collected exclusively for legitimate commercial and engineering purposes:

  1. Project Scoping & Feasibility: To analyze whether your requested features fit standard fixed packages or require custom architectural planning.
  2. Proposal & Quotation Delivery: To generate transparent written scope documents, milestone estimates, and contractual agreements.
  3. Direct Communication: To respond to your questions, coordinate onboarding sessions, and deliver scheduled progress updates.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade client contact details to third-party data brokers, marketers, or advertisers.

04

How Enquiries Are Processed & Transmitted

The website form sends your enquiry to a same-origin server endpoint. The enquiry is recorded in PostgreSQL together with a notification request. The team notification is processed separately, so a submission receipt confirms that the enquiry was received and recorded; it does not confirm that an email was delivered. During local development, synthetic notifications are captured by Mailpit and are not sent as real email.

A. Website enquiry form

Submitting this form sends the details to the website’s same-origin Fastify endpoint for server-side validation and recording. The site shows a receipt only after the enquiry and its notification request have been saved. The team notification is handled separately, and the receipt does not guarantee email delivery. In local development, Mailpit captures synthetic notifications for testing; it is not a public email provider.

B. Separate direct contact options

The email and WhatsApp links shown on the site are separate ways to contact us. They do not submit or change the website form.

C. WhatsApp Direct Channel

When you click "Chat via WhatsApp Direct", you are redirected to WhatsApp's official API (wa.me) to initiate an end-to-end encrypted direct dialogue with our studio. WhatsApp's own privacy policy governs data transmitted within their platform.

05

External Services & Contact Links

The site references these external services and optional contact routes:

  • Google Fonts: The site requests typefaces from Google Fonts.
  • Direct email and WhatsApp links: These are optional contact routes separate from the website form. Information you choose to send through those services is handled under their respective privacy terms.
  • Local development: Synthetic team notifications are captured by Mailpit, a local development tool and not a public email provider.
[Requires Owner Confirmation] The local V1 form does not use an external form-delivery service. Public hosting and production email services have not been configured or verified. Before public launch, the business owner must confirm the actual providers, their purposes, and any required privacy information. No external CRM or analytics SDK is configured in this codebase.
06

Data Retention & Deletion Rights

We retain contact form submissions and correspondence only for the duration required to evaluate inquiries, deliver active project scopes, and fulfill administrative records.

You maintain the right to inspect, correct, or request the permanent deletion of your contact data from our active records at any time.

How to Request Data Deletion:

To request complete deletion of your past inquiries and communications, email us at aevumstack@gmail.com with the subject line: Data Deletion Request - [Your Name / Business]. We process verified requests promptly.

[Requires Owner Confirmation]: Business owner must confirm whether a specific statutory archival schedule (e.g. inquiry retention schedule and accounting record retention in compliance with applicable local laws) applies to client communications.
07

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

AEVUM STACK prioritizes clean web engineering without surveillance bloat:

  • We do not set tracking, profiling, or behavioral advertising cookies.
  • We do not employ third-party remarketing pixels or fingerprinting scripts.
  • This local V1 configuration runs on loopback during development. Any additional logging or processing by a future public hosting provider depends on its configuration and should be reviewed before public launch.
